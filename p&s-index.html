— Why It Exists —

It started with
Dr. Piper.

At 17, he stood up at graduation in Sharon, Wisconsin and said men should never sell their influence to the highest bidder. Then he spent his life as a country vet who took chickens and handshakes instead of money.

He collected everything. Benjamin Franklin stoves. Spool beds. A lifetime of things that mattered.

When he died, the family held an auction. They walked away with $1,200.

The things didn't have stories attached. So they sold for nothing.
