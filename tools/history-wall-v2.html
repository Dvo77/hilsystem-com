The Tractor That Never Left
"Purchased in 1939 for $1,050. My great-grandfather financed $700 and his daughter Doris put up the $350 cash down payment. It has never left the property. It still runs."

Dr. Harry Benjamin Piper's 1939 Ford 9N sat in the same machine shed in Sharon, Wisconsin for 87 years. Fully restored by his descendants and retained as a working historical artifact. The HL address it carries — SH-MM-S2-M0 — is the same physical location it has occupied since the day it arrived on the farm.
