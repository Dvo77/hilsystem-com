Why This Exists
"Nothing is Lost. Everything is Logic."

People don't keep objects. They keep memories, possibilities, identity, and stories. But those things disappear — in estate sales, in dumpsters, in the brains of the people who cared about them and then died. HIL exists because every death is a catastrophic information loss event. This wiki is how you stop that.

Write the life manual that tells the whole story. Document what you built, what you know, where things are, and why they matter. The format is structured so any AI can read it, any person can navigate it, and any machine can act on it. That's not a feature — that's the point.

The Address Grammar
[ STRUCT ] — [ ZONE ] — [ ANCHOR ][ COL ] — [ LEVEL ][ DEPTH ]
Segment What it means Example
STRUCTThe building or structureSH = Shop · BM = Basement · GR = Garage
ZONERoom or sub-area within the structureMM = Metal Shop · OF = Office · KT = Kitchen
ANCHOR + COLWall direction + column number (left→right)S3 = South wall, 3rd column from left
LEVELVertical position (A = bottom, counts up)B = second shelf from floor
DEPTHHow far inside (0 = visible surface)0 = front · 2 = two layers back
SH-MM-S3-B0 Shop → Metal Shop zone → South wall → Column 3 → Second shelf → Surface visible
Your Data Promise
🔐
Your content syncs to your GitHub repo — not just this server.
Every page you write, every edit you make, every page you delete — your repo has the full history. When you're ready to run your own Wiki.js, point it at that repo and everything migrates cleanly. Zero data loss, full history intact. We're temporary. Your data is permanent.
📡
The format is the portability.
STD-WIKI-001 pages are plain markdown with structured frontmatter. They work in Wiki.js, Obsidian, Notion, a text editor, or a GitHub repo directly. No proprietary lock-in. The grammar travels wherever the files go — including into a local AI running on your own hardware.
🌱
The Incubator is a launchpad, not a destination.
When you're ready to own your own infrastructure, HIL will help you get there. Self-host on a home server, a TrueNAS box, or a VPS — your wiki goes with you. That's the graduation path, and it's built into the model from day one.
