HL SYSTEM — HUMAN LAYER

Set Up Without AI

No app. No internet.
No account required.
Just labels and logic.
1

Pick Your Structure Code

What building or area are you organizing? Choose a 2-letter code. Common ones on the reference card. Or make your own.

Shop → SH    House → HM    Garage → GR    Barn → BN
2

Post Your Cardinal Signs

Cut out the N, S, E, W signs from Page 1. Tape to the corresponding walls. Every address references these walls.

North sign → North wall    Phone compass works fine if unsure.
3

Name Your Zones

Divide your space into areas. Each gets a 2-letter zone code. Post the zone sign from Page 2.

Workshop → WS    SH-WS = Shop, Workshop zone
4

Number Your Wall Columns

Standing at the anchor wall, number shelf columns left to right: N1, N2, N3. Every position now has a unique address.

SH-WS-N2 = Shop, Workshop zone, North wall, column 2
5

Add Level and Write Your Labels

Add shelf level: T (top), M (middle), B (bottom), F (floor). Name items noun-first on the Avery labels. Stick them on.

TOOL-DRILL-CORDLESS → SH-WS-N2-M0
FASTENER-SCREW-WOOD → SH-WS-S1-B1
Item name first. Address second. That's the whole system.
