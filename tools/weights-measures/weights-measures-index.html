WEIGHTS & MEASURES

Reading a real tool correctly is its own skill, separate from the math behind it — a tape measure, a protractor, a caliper all fail you the same way if you can't actually read the marks. Start here to build tool literacy: what to reach for, how precise it actually is, and how to read it without guessing.

Outside, inside, depth, and step measurement — the same tool reading four different features depending on which jaws you use. Digital, dial, and vernier variants planned.

MICROMETER SIMULATOR

Sleeve and thimble reading, with a digital readout available as a checking aid — not a replacement for learning to read the mechanical scale itself.

SCALE SIMULATOR

roadmap · push, pull & platform scales · not yet built

Three different scale mechanisms read three different ways — a pull scale (hanging/spring, like a fish scale), a push scale (compression, like a bathroom scale), and a platform/balance scale. Volume and the fluid-ounce-vs-ounce mistake are a likely follow-on, not part of this build.

Ruler Simulator and Protractor Simulator are both content-complete prototypes reusing Calculator Explorer's Learn/Practice chassis — mode toggle, tap-to-explain, real grading engines. Both engines (the ruler's fraction parser, the protractor's degree/π-fraction formatter) were unit-tested standalone against known values before being wired into the UI, the same discipline Calculator Explorer's expression parser and Punctuation Explorer's answer bank used. Neither is wired to hil-shell.js or session-logger.js yet — same gap Punctuation Explorer has, and the same porting work: mechanical at this point, not a design question. Scale Rule Simulator from the original proposal isn't on this roadmap yet — it teaches a genuinely different skill (reading a scaled drawing, not a physical tool) and deserves its own scoping pass rather than being assumed into this list.

Weather Spotters is relocating into this folder from the Guild root (thermometers and pressure readings are measurement-tool skills same as everything else here) — its Guild top-level card stays for now, updated to point at the new path. Its badges (weather-watcher, junior-meteorologist) stay defined in hil-guild.html's catalog unchanged; whether they get re-categorized as Weights & Measures badges is still an open call, deferred rather than decided. Weather Spotters is also NOT added to WM_MODULE_IDS below — its own session-log/badge tracking is a separate, already-working system, and folding it into this hub's progress count would be guessing at an answer to the same open badge question rather than actually resolving it.
