— Public or Private —

You decide
what leaves the house.

Private by Default
  • Household inventory
  • Rooms and property details
  • Private family or team records
  • Kits and vessels
  • Receipts
  • Insurance information
  • Learning evidence
  • Account records

Private household records stay tied to your account and are not automatically exposed by joining HIL or connecting with another member.

Shared Deliberately
  • Selected Museum entries
  • Approved History Wall posts
  • Exchange listings
  • Public shared pages
  • League or Guild participation, where selected

Public content is created intentionally. Registration or membership never automatically reveals private inventory or property information.

No advertising-driven household profiling.

— Open HL System —

An open grammar
for physical location.

HL is the spatial location grammar underneath HIL. It gives structures, zones, walls, levels, and positions a consistent, machine-readable form. The grammar can be used independently — the HIL platform adds accounts, records, tools, relationships, and services around it.

— Why It Exists —

It started with Dr. Piper.

He was a country vet in Sharon, Wisconsin who spent a lifetime collecting things that mattered to him — and he took chickens and handshakes instead of money.

When he died, the family held an auction. They walked away with $1,200. The things didn't have stories attached. So they sold for nothing.

PROVENANCE RECORD — ACTIVE
Old Granddad Cabinet
OLG-1 — Shop / North Wall / Cabinet 1
Army-green metal hardware cabinet. Dr. Harry Benjamin Piper, veterinarian, Sharon WI. A lifetime of collected things, passed to family. Status: preserved.

HIL exists so that doesn't happen to your family's things. Every object you own can carry its story forward — not just its price tag.
