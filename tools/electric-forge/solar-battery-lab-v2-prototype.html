AzimuthSplitting east and west doesn't just shrink the noon spike — it reshapes the day into two smaller peaks (morning + afternoon) with a dip at solar noon, because neither half of the array is ever pointed straight at the sun.
ClippingWhen raw generation exceeds your inverter limit, the excess is chopped flat — overpaneling captures early/late sun at the expense of noon waste.
C-RateCurrent divided by amp-hour capacity verifies your storage bank can handle the charge rate safely without thermal degradation.
Calculator Keys For This Lab
C4 ×Wh = Ah × V, and Raw Solar = panel × sun% × efficiency × orientation — every step in this lab that combines two numbers into one bigger one uses Multiply.
E5 %Sun conditions, controller efficiency, and the orientation factor are all percentages cutting into the panel's rated output.
B4 −Net Flow = Actual Output − Load. Subtract tells you whether you're charging or draining.
D4 ÷Used twice: Net ÷ Voltage gets you current, then Current ÷ Ah gets you the C-rate.
