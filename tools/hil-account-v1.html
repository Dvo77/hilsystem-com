What HIL Collects
When you use HIL, we store the information you give us: your name and email from Google sign-in, the items you add to your inventory, photos you upload, receipt emails you forward, and your household settings. That's it. We don't collect anything you don't explicitly send us.
What We Do With It
Power your inventory, history, and exchange listings.
Run the email ingestion pipeline to help you capture receipts faster.
Generate AI-assisted features (PATCH) using your own data to answer your own questions.
Send you platform notifications you've opted into.
What We Don't Do
We do not sell your data. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.
We do not use your inventory data to train AI models.
We do not show ads based on what you own or what you've bought.
We do not share your data with third parties except the infrastructure providers that run HIL (Google Firebase, Cloudflare) — and they're bound by their own privacy policies.
Staff Access — The Honest Version
We built HIL so we don't have to look at your stuff. And honestly? We prefer it that way.

Your inventory data is partitioned to your account. The platform is architected so that casual browsing of your data by HIL staff isn't possible through normal tools — it would require a deliberate, non-trivial technical action.

If we ever access your account for a support reason, we log it. In the future, you'll see those access events right here in your account panel. If we ever have to do it without your knowledge, we'll tell you after the fact.

We're not interested in what you own. We're interested in building a platform that helps you keep track of it.
Government Requests
If we receive a legal demand for your data, we'll notify you unless a court order prevents it. We'll push back on overbroad requests. We keep a record of any government access to user data.
